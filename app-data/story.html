UNB's annual District Correspondents' Conference puts onus on credible journalism

Daylong engagements on different aspects of media landscape inaugurated by Zahir Uddin Swapon

UNB News August 22, 2026, 08:17 PM 12,418 views
Information and Broadcasting Minister Zahir Uddin Swapon speaks at the inaugural session of UNB's annual District Correspondents' Conference in Dhaka on Saturday. — Photo: UNB

UNB's annual District Correspondents' Conference-2026 began in the capital on Saturday, putting the emphasis on credible, fact-based journalism at a time when misinformation travels faster than ever across digital platforms.

Information and Broadcasting Minister Zahir Uddin Swapon inaugurated the daylong event as the chief guest, where district correspondents from across the country joined newsroom members for high-level panel discussions and interactive sessions.

Speaking at the inaugural session, Swapon said regional correspondents remain the backbone of a wire service, as they bring stories from places the national media rarely reaches.

"Credible journalism begins at the district level. If the first draft of a story is accurate, the whole ecosystem benefits." — Zahir Uddin Swapon

The conference featured sessions on verification tools, safety of journalists in the field, election coverage planning, and the use of multimedia — photos and video — to make wire copy more valuable to subscribing newsrooms.

UNB editors said the agency will expand its photo and video distribution packages this year so that district coverage can be packaged with relevant media for clients.

Awards were also handed out to the best district correspondents of the past year, recognising exclusive reporting from remote districts.
